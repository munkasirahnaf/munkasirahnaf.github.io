Academic Projects & Thesis

Molecular Simulation Study on Adsorption of CO2 in Siliceous Zeolites

My undergraduate thesis was to study the adsorption behaviour of siliceous zeolites, which can be used as selective adsorbents for CO2 separation. Zeolites are a versatile class of materials that can display selective CO2 adsorption behavior and thus find applications in carbon capture, utilization, and storage (CCUS). Molecular simulations were conducted in Cassandra, an open source Monte Carlo package developed in the Maginn group at the University of Notre Dame.

  • Cassandra & MoSDeF Cassandra: for molecular simulations
  • Matplotlib & seaborn: for visualization
  • LaTeX & TikZ: for typesetting
